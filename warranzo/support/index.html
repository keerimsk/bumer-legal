Support

Write to founder@bumer.app. The person who makes warranzo reads every email.

If something isn’t working, tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you tapped just before.

Signing in

I didn’t get a sign-in code

Codes come by email from bumer.app and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or junk folder. A code works once and expires after 10 minutes, so ask for a new one in the app if it’s late.

How do I get my receipts on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same email address. Your backed-up receipts come back, and the photos fill in as they arrive.

Locked receipts, health-looking receipts and receipts warranzo didn’t read aren’t backed up, so they don’t come back. Export them from the old iPhone first: Settings, then Export all.

What happens when I sign out?

Your receipts stay in your account, and this iPhone’s copy is removed. Receipts that are only on this iPhone are removed too.

Dates and claims

Are the return and warranty dates certain?

Return windows and warranty periods are suggestions based on your receipt and common store and maker terms. Check with the store or maker to confirm.

warranzo keeps your proof and your dates. Returns and warranty claims are between you and the store or the maker. Some stores and makers ask for the original paper receipt. A photo helps, but it isn’t a guarantee.

My reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications for warranzo in your iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications. Then check the reminder switches in warranzo’s Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work without a connection.

Pro and purchases

What’s free?

warranzo reads your first 10 receipts for free. Reminders and search are always free. Pro reads unlimited receipts and makes proof packs.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Settings, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel Yearly?

Open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then warranzo. Or go to your App Store subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel it. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there’s nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I get a copy of everything?

Settings, then Export all. You get every receipt as a spreadsheet, with the photos, in one file.

What’s the difference between Delete everything and Delete account?

Delete everything (Settings) deletes every receipt on this iPhone and in your backup. Your account stays, empty.

Delete account (Settings, then Account) deletes your account and every receipt, photo and reminder in it, on this iPhone and on our server. It can’t be undone.

What we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy.
