Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

warranzo is an iPhone app that keeps your receipts and the return and warranty dates on them. It’s made by Abdulkerim Can Sakız, an independent developer (“we” in this policy). This page explains what warranzo keeps, where, for how long, and how you delete it.

Questions: founder@bumer.app

The short version

  • You sign in with your email address and a 6-digit code. There’s no password.
  • Your receipts are backed up to your account, so they come back on a new iPhone.
  • Receipts that look health-related, and receipts you lock with Face ID, are never backed up. They stay on your iPhone.
  • warranzo reads receipts on your iPhone. No outside service reads them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. We don’t sell or share your data.
  • You can delete a receipt, everything, or your whole account inside the app.

What we keep in your account

Your account

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes.
  • An account ID we assign.
  • When the account was created and when you last signed in.

The receipts you keep

  • What warranzo reads from a receipt: the store, the brand, the main item (not every line), the price, the purchase date, the model and serial number when they’re printed or you add them, and the return and warranty dates with where they came from.
  • The receipt’s text, so search works. Card numbers are masked in it, last digits included.
  • Your notes, and whether you marked an item “returned it” or “all good”.
  • The photo of the receipt, its cleaned-up legible copy, and PDFs you import. A PDF is stored under the receipt’s number; its original file name isn’t kept.

A receipt photo is stored as you took it. Anything printed on the paper, such as the last digits of a card, stays in the photo.

Settings that follow you

  • Your four reminder switches.
  • How many receipts warranzo has read for you, for the free limit of 10. This number only goes up, so the limit works the same on a new phone. Details you type in yourself don’t count.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Health-looking receipts, for example from a pharmacy or a clinic. warranzo recognizes them on your iPhone, doesn’t read their items and doesn’t keep their text. Our servers refuse them.
  • Receipts you lock with Face ID. If you lock a receipt that was already backed up, its copy on our servers is deleted.
  • Receipts warranzo didn’t read, such as a photo kept past the free limit, unless you turn on “Back this up” for that receipt.
  • Reading. Text recognition runs on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in tools. Nothing is sent to an outside reading service.
  • Purchases. Apple handles payment and we never see your card. Your Pro status is read from Apple on your iPhone.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. The Closing soon widget reads from the app on the same iPhone.
  • The sample receipt, and app settings such as “Hide prices on shared cards”.

Signing out removes this iPhone’s copy of your receipts. The ones that are only on this iPhone go with it, so export them first if you want to keep them.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To back up your receipts and keep your iPhones in sync.
  • To apply the free limit and your Pro plan.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We don’t use your data for advertising. We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t sell or share your personal information, and we don’t use your receipts to train AI models.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase runs our database, file storage and sign-in. Your account and backed-up receipts are stored in Supabase’s Canada (Central) region, encrypted in storage and in transit. Supabase handles them only on our instructions.
  • Hostinger sends the emails with your sign-in codes, from bumer.app.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases under its own terms.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Until you delete them
A deleted receipt’s blank mark
90 days

Its contents are erased at once. The mark holds none of the receipt’s details, just its ID, times and sync markers, so your other iPhones learn it’s gone.

Photos and PDFs of a deleted receipt
Right away

The app deletes them with the receipt. If it can’t reach our servers at that moment, they’re deleted when you delete your account.

A locked receipt’s blank mark
While your account exists

Locking a backed-up receipt deletes its copy on our servers. The mark holds none of its details, just its ID, times and sync markers, so your other iPhones keep it locked.

Supabase also keeps short-lived technical logs of requests to our servers, which can include an IP address, for a limited time set by its service. Hostinger, which delivers your sign-in codes, keeps its own logs of the emails it sends.

Deleting your data

  • A receipt: open it, then Delete receipt.
  • Everything: Settings, then Delete everything. This deletes every receipt on this iPhone and in your backup, so your other iPhones lose them too. Your account stays.
  • Your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Your account, every backed-up receipt and file, your settings and your sessions are deleted from our servers right away, and the app on that iPhone is cleared. This can’t be undone.
  • By email: write to founder@bumer.app from the address you sign in with, and we’ll delete your account for you.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Yearly subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings; Apple keeps billing until you do.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Settings, then Export all. You get every receipt as a spreadsheet, with the photos, in one file.
  • Correct it: edit any receipt.
  • Delete it: as above.

You can also write to us for a copy of your data or to have it deleted. If we can’t do what you ask, we’ll tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.

Some US state laws give these rights by law. We give them to everyone, wherever you live. An account is needed for backup and sync; to stop all collection, delete your account.

Security

  • Each account can reach only its own receipts and files. The database enforces this.
  • Photos and PDFs sit in private storage.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and work once.
  • Data is encrypted in storage and in transit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Children

warranzo isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we’ll delete it.

Do Not Track

We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, so we don’t change anything in response to Do Not Track signals.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
