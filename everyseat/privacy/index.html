Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

everyseat plans meals. It doesn’t give medical advice, and it can be wrong. Always check labels.

everyseat plans one dinner for everyone at your table, keeping each person’s allergies, diets and no-thanks. It’s made by Abdulkerim Can Sakız, an independent developer (“we” in this policy). This page explains what everyseat keeps, who sees it, for how long, and how you delete it.

Questions: founder@bumer.app

The short version

  • You sign in with your email address and a 6-digit code. There’s no password.
  • Your table, including each seat’s allergies and diets, is kept in your everyseat account on our servers. That keeps it backed up and on every iPhone you sign in to.
  • The app asks for your agreement before you enter any allergy or diet.
  • We use it only to plan dinners. It’s not a medical record. We never sell it or use it for ads.
  • People you invite to plan with you see every seat and rule at your table.
  • Delete a seat, leave a table, or delete your account anytime in the app.

Your health data

Each seat’s allergies (the major food allergens and any you add) and diets are health data. Some US state laws treat them that way, and so do we.

  • Where it comes from: you, and the people who plan at your table with you.
  • Why we keep it: to check every ingredient of every dinner and list against each seat’s rules. Nothing else.
  • Your agreement: the app asks before you enter any. Until you agree, it shows an example table. We keep when you agreed and to which version of the text.
  • Who sees it: the people who plan at your table, and Supabase, which stores it for us (below). Nobody else. We never sell it and never use it for ads.
  • Taking it back: delete the rules from a seat, delete the seat, or delete your account. Deleting a seat erases its rules right away. You can also write to us.

What we keep in your account

Your account

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes.
  • An account ID we assign, and when you signed up and last signed in.
  • When you agreed to the health data text, and to which version, and when you accepted the “not medical advice” notice.

Your table

  • Each seat’s name or nickname. A kid seat needs only a nickname; an age range (kid, teen or adult) is optional.
  • Each seat’s allergies, diets and no-thanks, its color and its place at the table, and when a guest seat ends.
  • The table’s name and settings: dinners per week, no oven, longest cooking time.

Your weeks

  • The dinners planned for each week and the swaps used.
  • What you checked off on the grocery list.
  • Favorites, dinners you asked not to see again, and each seat’s reactions to a dinner.

Planning together

  • Who plans at your table and who owns it.
  • Invites: only a scrambled form of each code, when it expires and whether it was used. We never keep the code itself.
  • That you agreed to share your table, and to which version of the text.

everyseat Plus

If you subscribe, we keep the plan, Apple’s transaction IDs and when the subscription ends. Our servers check Apple’s signature on it before Plus lifts your table’s limits. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card.

Rule break reports

When you flag a dinner, we keep the date, the dinner, the rules you think it broke, your note and the app version. No names are sent. A copy stays on your iPhone.

Only on your iPhone

  • Your planning and grocery day reminders. They’re scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Your iPhone keeps your latest 12 weeks. Older weeks stay in your account.

Who sees it

  • People who plan at your table. Only the table’s owner invites them. They see every seat’s allergies, diets and no-thanks, the kids’ too, and the same week and list. They can add rules. Only the owner removes a rule, a seat with rules, or a person.
  • Supabase runs our database and sign-in. Your account and table are stored in Supabase’s US East (N. Virginia) region, encrypted in storage and in transit. Supabase handles them only on our instructions.
  • Hostinger sends the emails with your sign-in codes, from bumer.app.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases under its own terms.

No ads, trackers or analytics tools, and no outside AI service sees your table. We don’t sell or share your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, table, weeks and reports: until you delete them or your account.
  • A deleted seat: its name, allergies, diets and no-thanks are erased right away. A blank mark with only its ID and time stays for 90 days so your other iPhones learn it’s gone. Deleted favorites, reactions and list checks leave the same kind of mark for 90 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: they end after 180 days unused, and 365 days after sign-in at most. Then the app asks you to sign in again.
  • The IP address and device details of a session: not kept.
  • Sign-in history in our database: not kept.
  • An email that asked for a code but never used it: deleted after 1 day.
  • Your Plus record: deleted with your account.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes, one use.
  • Invites: a code works once and expires after 7 days.
  • Database backups: none kept today.

Supabase also keeps short-lived technical logs of requests to our servers, which can include an IP address, for a limited time set by its service. We don’t read them. Hostinger, which delivers your sign-in codes, keeps its own logs of the emails it sends.

Deleting your data

  • A seat: delete it from your table. If others plan with you, only the owner can delete a seat that has rules.
  • Leave a table: in Plan together. The table’s data then leaves your iPhone.
  • This iPhone only: Settings, then Delete everything on this iPhone. Your account keeps your table.
  • Your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything in it, health data included, from our servers and from this iPhone. It can’t be undone. If other people plan at your table, the app asks whether to leave the table with them or delete it for everyone.
  • By email: write to founder@bumer.app from the address you sign in with, and we’ll delete your account for you.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an everyseat Plus subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings; Apple keeps billing until you do.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Settings, then Export my table.
  • Correct it: edit any seat.
  • Delete it: as above.

You can also write to us for a copy of your data or to have it deleted. If we can’t do what you ask, we’ll tell you why, and you can ask us to look again. These choices are for everyone, wherever you live.

Not medical advice

everyseat plans meals. It doesn’t give medical advice, and it can be wrong.

  • Dinners come from a library of checked recipes. Every ingredient is checked against each seat’s rules.
  • An ingredient everyseat can’t match is marked “not sure”. It’s never counted as fitting a rule.
  • We make no promise that a dinner is free of any allergen. Products, labels and recipes change, and a recipe doesn’t list what a product may have touched. Check every label, every time, and follow your doctor’s advice.

If someone has a severe reaction, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Security

  • Only the people at a table can reach its data. The database enforces this.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and work once. A password can’t open an everyseat account.
  • Data is encrypted in storage and in transit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Children

everyseat is for adults who plan meals for a household; children don’t use it themselves. A kid seat holds only a nickname, an optional age range and the kid’s food rules, entered by a parent or caregiver. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn a child has signed up, we’ll delete the account.

Do Not Track

We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, so we don’t change anything in response to Do Not Track signals.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
