Support

If someone has a severe reaction, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Write to founder@bumer.app. The person who makes everyseat reads every email.

If something isn’t working, tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you tapped just before. Please don’t send allergy details you don’t need to.

Dinners and rules

What does “not sure” mean?

everyseat couldn’t match an ingredient in that recipe against your rules, so it may contain something a seat can’t eat. Check the label or swap the dinner. Swapping a not-sure dinner doesn’t use one of your swaps.

If you checked the label yourself, tap “I checked the label”. The dinner stays and is still marked not sure.

A dinner breaks a rule

Open the dinner and tap Flag a Rule Break. Pick the rule it doesn’t keep and add a note if you like. The dinner is removed from your table and won’t be suggested again. Your report goes to the everyseat team for review, and a copy stays on your iPhone.

Is everyseat medical advice?

No. everyseat plans meals. It doesn’t give medical advice, and it can be wrong. Always check labels. See Not medical advice in the privacy policy.

What’s always free?

Every check stays free: the rules on each dish, “not sure”, Why this dish? and flagging a mistake. So do editing your table, exporting it and deleting it.

Signing in

I didn’t get a sign-in code

Codes come by email from bumer.app and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or junk folder. A code works once and expires after 10 minutes, so ask for a new one in the app if it’s late.

How do I get my table on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same email address. Your table, your weeks and your list come back.

What happens when I sign out?

Your table stays in your account and comes back when you sign in. It leaves this iPhone.

Planning together

How do I plan with someone else?

The table’s owner opens Plan together and creates an invite code. A code works once and expires after 7 days. Two people can plan a free table.

Everyone who plans at your table sees every seat’s allergies, diets and no-thanks, the kids’ too, and the same week and list. They can add rules. Only the owner removes a rule, a seat with rules, or a person.

How do I leave a table or remove someone?

In Plan together. When you leave, the table’s data leaves your iPhone. The owner can remove anyone, anytime.

everyseat Plus

What does Plus add?

Without Plus, everyseat plans 3 dinners a week. Plus plans all 7, with unlimited swaps, search, favorites, past weeks and a guest seat.

How do I restore Plus on a new iPhone?

Tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then everyseat. Or go to your App Store subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I get a copy of my table?

Settings, then Export my table.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and everything in it, health data included, from our servers and from this iPhone. It can’t be undone. If others plan at your table, the app asks whether to leave the table with them or delete it for everyone.

To clear only this iPhone, use Delete everything on this iPhone in Settings. Your account keeps your table.

What we keep and for how long is in the privacy policy.
